**Goal:** Transplant the two things that produced gains in jax-Ka's GQT line: (1) a dedicated **pair head** over the shared encoder trained with a within-series listwise loss (anchor ligand vs candidate ligands of the same series with soft potency targets, plus the arm-A/C negatives as zero-target candidates), leaving the affinity head on its pointwise MSE; (2) **auxiliary heads** that regress the pocket and ligand 3D Zernike descriptors from the pooled embedding, so the encoder is pushed to carry pocket shape/property content (the pocket aux cannot be satisfied by a ligand-only model).

**Architecture:** `src/heads.py` gains `PairHead` and `MomentsHead`; `train_paired.py` gains `--pair-head`, `--pair-weight`, `--pair-neg-kinds`, `--moments {none,ligand,pocket,both}`, `--moments-weight`; the dataset gains a side array of descriptors aligned to `row`; the evaluator gains pair-head ranking (reusing the arm-S pairwise machinery with a generic scorer) and moments R².

**Spec:** user instruction "then move on to the pair head + ligand/pocket moments as aux tasks"; GQT reference: `aux_redge` pair head `MLP([z_a ⊙ z_c, |z_a − z_c|])` and the within-complex listwise loss `L = −Σ_c q_c log softmax_c ℓ(z_a, z_c)` with `q = t / Σt` (jax-Ka snapshot `hotspot-mask-v2/.../production_residue.py:434-443`), aux heads at weight 1 (burial) in the GQT default recipe.

## Global Constraints (in addition to the earlier plans)

- **Pair head:** `PairHead(dim=1536, hidden=512)`: `ℓ(z_a, z_c) = MLP([z_a ⊙ z_c, |z_a − z_c|, z_a − z_c])` → scalar (the third block makes it asymmetric, needed for "c more potent than a"; LayerNorm, leaky_relu, two hidden layers).
- **Listwise loss (per anchor a in the batch, MSE-kind rows only as anchors):** candidates = all other MSE-kind rows of the same series in the batch with a different ligand key (targets `t_c = softmax_c(y_c / τ)` with `τ = 1 pK / scale`, i.e. a potency-weighted relevance over the candidate set; anchors need ≥ 2 candidates) plus, when `--pair-neg-kinds` is non-empty, the anchor's own augmented negatives of those kinds present in the batch (target 0; twins kind 4, contact edits kind 6, decoys kind 2). `L_a = −Σ_c q_c log softmax_c ℓ(z_a, z_c)`, `q = t / Σ_c t` over candidates (negatives contribute 0 to q but are in the softmax). Mean over anchors; zero when none. Weight `--pair-weight` (default 0.3 as GQT's `within 0.3`; sweep 1.0).
- **Moments heads:** `MomentsHead(dim=1536, out=343)` (one hidden layer 512) ×2, MSE on standardised pocket F and ligand F (7×49 each; standardisation from the descriptor file's train rows), rows without descriptors masked out; `--moments-weight` (default 0.3; the GQT recipe uses 1 for burial). Pose/aug rows (kinds ≠ 0) use their parent's descriptors (same pocket; for twins (kind 4) the ligand descriptor is the twin's own, pocket the anchor's; for edits the ligand descriptor is unavailable → masked).
- **Descriptor side file:** `work/data_ac/paired_zernike.npz` with `row [N]`, `pocket_F [N,343] float32`, `ligand_F [N,343]`, `mask_pocket [N] bool`, `mask_ligand [N] bool`, `mean/sd` vectors (train rows). Built by a new `src/build_zernike_side.py --meta work/data_ac/paired_meta.csv --zernike work/data/zernike.npz --twins-meta work/data/twins_meta.csv --out ...` (twins: `twin_unique_id` gives the ligand descriptor). The trainer loads it once and indexes by `data.row`.
- **Checkpoint rule unchanged** (pointwise valid Pearson), so the aux heads can only help the primary head through the shared encoder; the pair head is evaluated as its own ranker.
- **Evaluation:** `--pairwise` extended: pair-head per-ligand score `s_i = mean_{a ≠ i} ℓ(z_a, z_i)` over the series (ensemble: mean of ℓ over models) → `kendall_pair`, `spearman_pair`, `pair_dir_acc` (sign of ℓ(z_a,z_c) − ℓ(z_c,z_a) vs sign of y_c − y_a) in `pairwise_summary.csv` with `head = pairhead`; negatives: on `aug_valid`, rank accuracy of the pair head (anchor's positive candidate vs its negatives) per negative kind in `aug_summary.csv` (`head = pairhead` rows). Moments: `moments_summary.csv` with per-split R² (pocket, ligand) for the aux heads, ensemble and per seed; ligand-only models report the same (expected: ligand R² fine, pocket R² near 0).
- Arms (3 seeds, data_ac, kinds 0 unless stated, reference `base-g`): `M` (moments both, 0.3), `P` (pair head 0.3), `PM` (both), `P1` (pair 1.0), `PMneg` (pair head with `--pair-neg-kinds 4,6`, kinds 0,3,4,5,6,7 in the data but hinge weight 0 so the negatives enter only the listwise term; MSE kinds as usual), gates `M-lig`, `PM-lig`.
