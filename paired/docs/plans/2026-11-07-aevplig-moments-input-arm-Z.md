Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Builds on arms S and P (`2026-11-05-aevplig-siamese-arm-S.md`, `2026-11-05-aevplig-pairhead-moments-arm-P.md`): the `AffinityWithSiamese` wrapper, the descriptor side file (`work/data_ac/paired_zernike.npz`, keys `row, pocket_F [N,343], ligand_F [N,343], mask_pocket, mask_ligand, mean/sd`), `build_zernike_side.py`, `featurise_zernike.py` (`--fep-dir` mode) and the evaluator are reused.

**Goal:** Test whether giving AEV-PLIG the rotation-invariant pocket and ligand property descriptors as *inputs* (not auxiliary targets) improves affinity prediction and ranking, with baselines that separate "the descriptors carry affinity signal on their own" from "the descriptors add to the graph+AEV encoder", and a ligand-only gate.

**Architecture:** `heads.py` gains `MomentsInput(d_in, proj)`: standardised descriptor vector → `Linear(d_in, proj) + leaky_relu + LayerNorm` → concatenated to the pooled embedding `z` (1536) → the affinity MLP's `fc1` is rebuilt with input `1536 + proj` (and the Siamese/pair heads, if used, keep reading `z` only). A `MomentsOnlyNet(d_in)` (MLP 343·k → 512 → 256 → 1, same activation/BatchNorm style as the clone's MLP) is the no-graph baseline. Descriptors are attached to every batch as `data.moments [B, d_in]` by the trainer/evaluator from a lookup keyed by `row` (paired datasets) or by `unique_id`/`graph_id` (FEP, 15-PGDH), with a per-row `missing` flag appended as the last input feature (descriptor zeros when missing).

**Spec:** user decision "Yes, moments as input" (2026-11-07) after the head arms; protocol: 3-seed screen on `series_valid` (3,591 rows) and `group_valid` (5,124 rows), 10 seeds on FEP for survivors.

## Global Constraints (in addition to earlier plans)

- `--input-moments {none,pocket,ligand,both,bothx}`: `pocket` = pocket F (343), `ligand` = ligand F (343), `both` = 686, `bothx` = 686 + cross block (343: elementwise product of the standardised pocket-complement vector and the standardised ligand vector, complement = channels 2↔3 and 5↔6 swapped, i.e. the complementarity features used for twin selection) → `d_in = 343·k + 1` (missing flag). `--input-moments-proj 256` (0 = raw concat). Standardisation = the side file's train mean/sd; the same mean/sd are applied to FEP and 15-PGDH descriptors.
- Descriptors for evaluation sets: `featurise_zernike.py --csv work/data/fep_benchmark.csv --out work/data/zernike_fep.npz` and `--csv work/data/pgdh.csv --out work/data/zernike_pgdh.npz` (new generic `--csv` mode: columns `unique_id, sdf_file, pdb_file`, same shell-mode descriptor as the training set; paths relative to `$WORK`; the structures already exist under `work/data/fep_structures/` and `work/data/pgdh_structures/`); the evaluator takes `--zernike-eval FILE[,FILE...]` and maps `unique_id` → descriptor for the FEP/pgdh datasets (the `_processed.csv` gives `graph_id` ↔ `unique_id`).
- Arms (data_ac, kinds 0, 3 seeds first): `Zp` (pocket), `Zl` (ligand), `Zb` (both), `Zbx` (bothx), `Zlin` (moments-only baseline, `--model moments_only`, bothx), `Zb-lig` (ligand-only encoder + both; the gate: if the pocket moments give the ligand-only model what the AEVs give the full model, pocket information in the AEV path is the redundant part), and `Zbx-lig`. Reference `base-g` (10 seeds exist) and `base-g-lig` (10 seeds exist).
- Screen metrics (paired per seed): `series_valid` within-series wSpearman (n ≥ 5) and pointwise Pearson, `group_valid` pointwise Pearson/RMSE and within-series wSpearman; secondary: CASF, FEP. Survivor rule: an arm goes to 10 seeds if its 3-seed paired mean on `group_valid` Pearson or `series_valid` wSpearman exceeds 2 se of that difference AND exceeds the corresponding ligand-only gate's gain. FEP decides at 10 seeds with the usual rule (paired mean > 2 se and > gate).
- Model files: the wrapper's state dict includes `moments_in` and the enlarged `fc1`; the sidecar records `input_moments`, `input_moments_proj`, `input_dim`, `zernike_side`, `zernike_eval`; `load_models` rebuilds accordingly; models without the option load as before. Saving a model with `--input-moments none` must be byte-identical to arm-S/P behaviour (test).
- 15-PGDH: `pgdh-<tag>` evals use `--zernike-eval work/data/zernike_pgdh.npz`.
- All earlier flags remain usable with `--input-moments` (e.g. a later `Zbx + pair head`), but arm Z runs them off.
